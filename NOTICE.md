# Picshoo

Picshoo combines two MIT-licensed macOS apps:

- [capcap](https://github.com/realskyrin/capcap) by realskyrin — the capture flow and the
  annotation editor. Its license is in `LICENSE`.
- [Tendedero](https://github.com/alejandrobujan/tendedero) by Alejandro Buján — the line of
  screenshots along the top of the screen (`capcap/Line/`). Its license is in
  `ThirdParty/Tendedero/LICENSE`. The Tendedero name and icon are not used.

Picshoo is not affiliated with or endorsed by either author.
