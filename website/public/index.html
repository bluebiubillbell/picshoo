Picshoo: screenshots, on the line

Picshoo is a free Mac app for macOS 14 or later that hangs every screenshot on a line along the top of your screen. Click a photo to copy it, drag it into an app or a folder, hold it to mark it up in capcap's editor, or click its cross to let it go. Double-tap ⌘ to capture. It also brings capcap's scrolling capture, beautify, pin to screen, text recognition, translation, history and image-host upload

Picshoo is stitched together from two MIT-licensed apps, capcap by realskyrin and Tendedero by Alejandro Buján
